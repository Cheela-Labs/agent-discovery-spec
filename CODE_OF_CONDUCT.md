# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project's issues, PRs, and discussions to be a place where
anyone — regardless of experience level, employer, or which implementation
they showed up defending — can propose an idea and have it judged on its
technical merits.

## Expected behavior

- Critique proposals, schemas, and code, not people.
- Assume good faith. Most disagreements here are "we optimized for different
  constraints," not bad intent.
- Disclose your interest. If you work for a company implementing this spec
  and a proposal affects your product, say so up front — it's not
  disqualifying, it's just relevant context.
- Keep technical debate in the open, on the issue or PR it belongs to.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Personal attacks, harassment, or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Sustained disruption of discussion (derailing, bad-faith arguing, sealioning).
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Attempting to steamroll process — e.g. merging normative changes without
  going through `Last Call`, or pressuring editors outside the public
  record.

## Scope

Applies to all project spaces: issues, PRs, discussions, and any other
official communication channel this project maintains.

## Enforcement

Report concerns by opening a private security advisory on the repository, or
by contacting a project editor directly. Editors will review and respond
within a reasonable time, and will keep the reporter's identity confidential
except where disclosure is required to resolve the report.

Consequences scale with severity and pattern: a private word, a public
warning, a temporary ban from project spaces, or a permanent one — decided by
editor consensus, same as any other governance decision in this project.
